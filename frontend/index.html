Media Sources

Explore the source framework behind India Media Intelligence. News is indexed from approved media sources and presented with source attribution so users can discover information with greater context and transparency.

◉

Approved Sources

Media sources are reviewed and configured through the platform's administrative source-management process before they are included in the indexed collection.

▤

RSS & Feed Based

Where available, structured feeds are used to collect article metadata efficiently and keep the indexed news collection current.

✓

Source Attribution

Every search result identifies its originating media source and provides a link to the original article where available.

Source Coverage

The platform is designed to organize Indian media coverage across multiple editorial and subject areas. Coverage can evolve as new approved sources and feeds are added by administrators.

National NewsRegional NewsEducationResearch & InnovationTechnologyGovernment & PolicyBusiness & EconomyCampus & Higher Education

How Source Governance Works

  • 1. Source IdentificationRelevant Indian media publications and information sources are identified for possible inclusion.
  • 2. Review & DecisionAdministrators record the source decision and ingestion status before activation.
  • 3. Feed ConfigurationRSS/feed endpoints are mapped to the appropriate source, language and category.
  • 4. IndexingApproved feed content is collected into the platform's searchable article index.
Source availability and article volume can change over time. The Admin Dashboard is the operational authority for source activation, feed configuration and ingestion status.

What the Platform Does

India Media Intelligence brings indexed media content into a single search experience. Users can search by organization, topic, location, education, policy, technology and other terms, then refine results using available filters.

⌕

Discover

Search across the indexed collection using natural-language queries and suggested searches.

◫

Filter

Refine results by date range, category, language and source to narrow the information set.

↗

Verify

Open the original article and review the source attribution before using information for further work.

Built for Responsible Discovery

  • Source-first approachSearch results retain the identity of the originating media source.
  • Controlled indexingSources and feeds are managed through administrative controls.
  • Structured metadataArticles can be organized by title, date, source, category and language.
  • Original-source accessUsers can follow the result to the publisher's original article.
